§ 01 — About

소개

About

안녕하세요, KAIST 수학문제연구회입니다. 저희는 1988년 창설된 유서깊은 동아리입니다. 수리과학과 외에도 다양한 학과의 회원들이 활동하고 있으며, 새내기부터 대학원생 선배까지 자유롭게 생각을 나누는 분위기가 조성되어 있습니다. 수학실력과는 무관하니 수학을 좋아하는 학생분들이라면 모두 환영합니다.

Translation

Hello, this is the KAIST Mathematical Problem Solving Group. Our club was founded in 1988, which is one of the clubs in KAIST. Members of various departments are active, regardless of undergraduate and graduates. We freely share our thoughts. Welcome all those who like math regardless of math skills!

역사

History

  1. 1988

    수학문제연구회 창립

    Club founded

    MSquare, KAIST Mathematical Problem Solving Group, founded at KAIST in 1988.

  2. ⋯ 24 Years · 1989–2011

    축적 생략 · axis break

  3. 2012–2013

    ML 발간

    Math Letter published

    Math Letter (ML), our publication of math articles, olympiad problems and reader solutions.

    2014–2023

    수학동아 연재

    Quiz King & KAIST

    A decade-long series with Math Donga, Korea's mathematics magazine for student readers.

    2023

    수학문제연구회 홈커밍

    Homecoming

    Seniors who had gone on to many careers came back to share what they had learned.

    2024

    Jane Street 스폰서십 체결

    Sponsored by Jane Street

    We signed a sponsorship with Jane Street.

    2025–

    카이스퀘어 대외 발행

    Public release of KAI-Square

    We began releasing KAI-Square, our magazine, to readers outside the club.
